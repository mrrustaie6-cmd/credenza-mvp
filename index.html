Process

From invisible to underwritable

Four steps, the same for every corridor — only the data adapters underneath change.

01 / Consent

Person opts in

The newcomer chooses exactly which sources to connect. Nothing is pulled without an explicit, revocable grant.

02 / Verify

Signals cross-checked

Documents, on-chain history, guarantors and registries are checked against each other, not taken at face value.

03 / Score

Trust profile assembled

A single, explainable index is produced — with a conservative starting limit in low-data corridors.

04 / Issue

Bank queries via API

The destination bank calls Credenza's API at the point of onboarding — no manual paperwork on either side.

Concept note

Why this, why now

Global credit-portability products already exist and already work — banks pay for them. What none of them can do is touch a sanctioned corridor. Every year, tens of thousands of Iranians alone move to Europe and the Americas carrying real, provable financial discipline that simply has nowhere to land. Credenza is built specifically for that gap, by someone who has spent years navigating it personally — not as an outside observer, but from inside Iran's banking system.

This page is a concept demonstration prepared alongside the written business plan for evaluation by Startup Estonia's Startup Committee. No real user data is collected here.
